# Lesson 30 — Phase 2 Interleaved Review: Subjunctive, Relative Clauses, Reported Speech, Conditional
### Phase 2: Expansion (A2 → B1) — Review Checkpoint

**Competency axes trained:** All Phase 2 axes so far, interleaved
**Purpose:** Spaced repetition and interleaving applied to Lessons 26–29, mixed out of order to force retrieval before Phase 2 continues.

---

## 1. Why This Checkpoint

Lessons 26–29 introduced four genuinely complex systems in quick succession: the subjunctive, relative clauses, reported speech, and the conditional. Each leans on Spanish intuition heavily, but each also has French-specific mechanics that need active retrieval, not just recognition, before we build further.

---

## 2. Mixed Retrieval Practice

**A. Subjunctive (Lesson 26)**
1. Give the subjunctive form of "être" for "je" and "nous."
2. Does "je pense que" take indicative or subjunctive? What about "je ne pense pas que"?

**B. Relative Clauses (Lesson 27)**
3. Choose qui or que: "Le film ___ j'ai vu était bon." / "L'homme ___ parle est mon voisin."
4. How does French express "whose," and which relative pronoun handles it?

**C. Reported Speech (Lesson 28)**
5. Convert to past reported speech: Il a dit: « Je suis fatigué. »
6. What does "qu'est-ce que" become in a reported question?

**D. Conditional (Lesson 29)**
7. What two components form the conditional?
8. Complete: "Si j'avais de l'argent, je ___ (voyager) plus."

---

## 3. Answer Key

1. que je sois; que nous soyons
2. "Je pense que" → indicative; "je ne pense pas que" → subjunctive
3. Le film que j'ai vu... / L'homme qui parle...
4. "Dont" — because possession/"whose" is expressed via "de," and dont replaces "de + noun."
5. Il a dit qu'il était fatigué. (présent → imparfait)
6. "Ce que"
7. Futur simple stem + imparfait endings
8. voyagerais

---

## 4. Combined Production Task

Write or say aloud 4 sentences, each combining at least two of this lesson's four systems:
- One sentence with subjunctive + relative clause
- One sentence with reported speech + conditional
- One sentence with a "si" hypothetical + relative clause
- One free-form sentence combining any two systems of your choice

Example model (don't copy exactly — build your own): "Je veux trouver quelqu'un qui puisse m'aider" (subjunctive + relative clause: "puisse" is subjunctive of pouvoir, triggered by "vouloir que" logic even in a relative clause context — an advanced combination worth attempting).
