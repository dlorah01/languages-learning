## 1. Continuing the Web (Intuitive Explanation)

## 2. Family 7: Emotions

| French | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| content(e) | happy/glad | slightly less strong than "heureux" |
| heureux/heureuse | happy | |
| triste | sad | |
| fâché(e) / en colère | angry | fâché = mildly annoyed; en colère = genuinely angry |
| inquiet/inquiète | worried | |
| fatigué(e) | tired | connects to Lesson 7's "avoir sommeil" (to be sleepy) |
| stressé(e) | stressed | |
| surpris(e) | surprised | |
| déçu(e) | disappointed | false-friend alert: looks like "deceived" in English, but means "disappointed" |

**Emotion structure recap (Lesson 7 callback):** most emotions use être + adjective (je suis content) OR avoir + noun (j'ai peur — "I have fear/I'm afraid") — knowing which pattern applies to which emotion word is itself a small vocabulary fact to absorb per word.

## 3. Family 8: Work & Professional Life

| French | English |
|---|---|
| le travail | work/job |
| le bureau | office/desk |
| la réunion | meeting |
| le collègue / la collègue | colleague |
| le patron / la patronne | boss |
| le salaire | salary |
| travailler | to work (verb) |
| gagner sa vie | to earn a living |
| démissionner | to resign |

**Collocation note:** "le bureau" cleverly means both "desk" (the object) and "office" (the room/workplace) — context disambiguates, similar to how English "office" can mean a room or an organizational role.

## 4. Family 9: House & Home

| French | English |
|---|---|
| la maison | house |
| l'appartement | apartment |
| la chambre | bedroom |
| la cuisine | kitchen |
| la salle de bain | bathroom |
| le salon | living room |
| la porte | door |
| la fenêtre | window |
| le loyer | rent |
| déménager | to move (house) |

**False friend flag:** "la chambre" means specifically "bedroom," not "room" in general (that's "la pièce") — a common trap since English "chamber" suggests a more general room.

---

## 5. Mental Model: Domains as Neighborhoods in the Same City

If Lesson 10 built a small starter neighborhood in your mental "city" of French vocabulary, this lesson adds three new neighborhoods (emotions, work, home) connected by roads (the grammar and structures you already know: être/avoir patterns, gender/articles, adjective agreement). Every new domain you add makes the whole city more navigable, because the roads — not just the neighborhoods — are what make retrieval fast.

---

## 6. Common Mistakes

**Spanish speakers:** applying "decepcionado" intuition correctly to "déçu" (this one transfers well, both mean disappointed — a rare case where the false-friend trap is actually for English speakers, not you); occasionally confusing "fâché" (mildly annoyed) with stronger anger, since Spanish "enfadado" can span a similar range but regional usage varies.

**English speakers:** translating "déçu" as "deceived" (false cognate trap) instead of "disappointed"; using "chambre" to mean any room instead of specifically "bedroom."

## 7. Progressively Harder Examples

1. Je suis content, mais un peu fatigué. — "I'm happy, but a little tired."
2. Elle a peur de son patron. — "She's afraid of her boss."
3. Nous avons déménagé dans un appartement avec deux chambres. — "We moved into an apartment with two bedrooms."
4. Il est déçu parce qu'il n'a pas eu la réunion qu'il voulait. — "He's disappointed because he didn't get the meeting he wanted."
5. Je vais démissionner parce que je suis stressé et que le salaire n'est pas bon. — "I'm going to resign because I'm stressed and the salary isn't good."
