## 1. Philosophy: Why This Program Is Different

Most French courses optimize for *passing a level*. This program optimizes for **rewiring your default language of thought**. That means three things structurally:

1. **Every lesson is trilingual-aware.** French is taught *through* the lens of Spanish (your deepest intuition) and English (your strong analytical bridge) — showing where the three Romance/Germanic threads converge, diverge, and trick you (faux amis, false cognates, structural transfer errors).
2. **Nothing is isolated.** Grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, and register are taught as one interconnected system, not separate tracks. A verb lesson always touches pronunciation, collocation, and register at once.
3. **Production starts on day one.** You don't wait until "enough grammar" to speak — every lesson has a speaking/thinking-in-French component from Lesson 1.

## 2. Competency Map: What "Native-Like Intuition" Actually Requires

To go from A1 to genuine mastery, you need growth across **8 competency axes**, not just "grammar + vocab":

| Axis | What it means | Your current state (A1) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Phonology** | Producing & perceiving French sounds, nasal vowels, liaison, rhythm, intonation | Untrained — Spanish/English phonology will interfere heavily |
| **2. Morphosyntax** | Grammar as a generative system (agreement, conjugation, word order) | Minimal — needs full foundation |
| **3. Lexicon** | Depth of vocabulary: word families, register, collocation, not just word lists | Minimal |
| **4. Pragmatics/Register** | Knowing *when* to say what — formal vs. informal, spoken vs. written | None yet |
| **5. Discourse competence** | Connecting sentences into coherent argument/narrative (connectors, cohesion) | None yet |
| **6. Listening decoding** | Parsing fast, connected native speech (elision, reduction) | None yet |
| **7. Productive fluency** | Real-time speaking/writing without translating | None yet |
| **8. Cultural/literary competence** | Humor, idiom, literature, film — the "invisible" layer of a language | None yet |

Every lesson in this program will tag which axes it develops, so you always know what "muscle" you're training.

## 3. The Spanish/English/French Triangle (used constantly)

Because you're a Spanish native with strong English, French sits in a unique position:

- **Spanish → French**: ~75-80% lexical similarity (shared Latin roots), similar grammatical gender system, similar subjunctive concept — but very different phonology and very different rhythm (syllable-timed Spanish vs. stress-timed/nasal French).
- **English → French**: Huge shared vocabulary (Norman French loanwords, ~30% of English lexicon), useful for abstract/academic vocabulary — but English syntax and lack of grammatical gender will *not* help you, and can mislead you on register (many French/English cognates differ in formality level).
- **French's real challenge for you**: not grammar (Spanish already gave you subjunctive, gender, mood intuition) — it will be **phonology** and **register/naturalness**. This program weights pronunciation and natural spoken French more heavily than a typical course would for a Spanish speaker.

## 4. Program Structure: 6 Phases

Each phase = roughly one CEFR-equivalent band, but taught with a mastery lens (not exam lens). Phases overlap deliberately — you'll revisit earlier material with new depth (spaced repetition by design).

### **PHASE 0 — Foundations (Lessons 1–5)**
Before any grammar: sound system, history of French, how it relates to Spanish/English, the IPA, the mental model for how French organizes a sentence.

### **PHASE 1 — Core System (≈A1→A2) (Lessons 6–25)**
Present tense system, être/avoir/faire/aller as the four pillars, gender & number, articles/determiners, basic negation & questions, core 300 high-frequency words as word families (not lists), pronunciation of every core sound, first discourse connectors, first shadowing routine.

### **PHASE 2 — Expansion (≈A2→B1) (Lessons 26–55)**
Full tense system (passé composé vs. imparfait — the conceptual aspect distinction, not a rule to memorize), pronouns (the trickiest zone for Spanish/English speakers: y, en, double pronouns), prepositions as concepts, first 15 high-value verbs in depth, register split (tu/vous, formal/informal registers), liaison & connected speech systematically, first authentic listening.

### **PHASE 3 — Fluency Build (≈B1→B2) (Lessons 56–90)**
Subjunctive mastery (leveraging your Spanish intuition explicitly), relative clauses, reported speech, conditionals, passive voice, remaining 20 high-frequency verbs, idioms by frequency tier, business/academic vocabulary tracks begin, thinking-in-French daily exercises intensify, first unadapted native media.

### **PHASE 4 — Native Naturalness (≈B2→C1) (Lessons 91–130)**
Nuance layer: near-synonyms and when each is natural, discourse markers and fillers real speakers use, humor and wordplay, advanced sentence architecture, technical/professional register for your field, extensive collocation training, literature entry point.

### **PHASE 5 — Mastery & Maintenance (≈C1→C2 and beyond)**
Literary and philosophical French, regional/sociolinguistic variation, advanced debate and argumentation, untranslated thinking, film/podcast without support, long-term spaced-repetition maintenance system, becoming self-correcting.

## 5. Lesson Anatomy (every lesson follows this template)

Each lesson file will include:

1. **Competency tags** (which of the 8 axes it targets)
2. **Intuitive explanation** (plain-language "why")
3. **Technical explanation** (linguistic mechanics)
4. **ES/EN/FR comparison** (transfer points, false friends, structural contrasts)
5. **Pronunciation notes** (IPA + practice)
6. **Mental model / visual analogy**
7. **Common mistakes** (Spanish-speaker-specific + English-speaker-specific)
8. **Progressively harder examples**
9. **Collocations & related expressions**
10. **Speaking/thinking practice** (shadowing, role-play, or thinking-in-French prompt)
11. **Mini quiz + active recall check**
12. **Spaced repetition flags** (what from earlier lessons to review today)
