# 🌍 Languages Learning

A personal, systems-based language-learning lab. Every curriculum here is hand-built phase by phase — sound system and sentence structure first, then grammar taught as systems rather than memorized rules, then vocabulary, then the native layer — aimed at real fluency, not phrasebook survival.
